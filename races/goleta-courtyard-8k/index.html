04Run light. You're back within the hour.

Camera disciplineCap mount or handheld are both fine; film 15–30 seconds at a time. The marsh trail has cyclists and dog walkers, so step aside before you stop to film.

WaterNo confirmed water on the route. For an easy hour, drinking before and after is enough; carry a small soft flask if it's warm.

ClothingCoastal mornings are cool and the bluffs are windy. A T-shirt plus a light layer you can tie round your waist.

Reserve rulesNCOS and Devereux Slough are restored habitat, and there is a snowy plover protection area near Coal Oil Point. Stay on the trail and give birds space.
